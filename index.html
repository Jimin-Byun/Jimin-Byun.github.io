Jimin Byun

My work looks at what happens to a dancer’s movement when it passes through motion capture, avatars and AI.

  • MA Cultural Contents, Korea University, 2026
  • BA Dance, Pusan National University, 2024
Research interest
Observed

When I turned my own dancing into pose data (YOLO11, MediaPipe), the timing and speed of each phrase carried over. Breath in the torso and the articulation of the hands and feet did not, because these pose-estimation tools place no points on the chest or along the fingers and toes.

Question

As movement is mediated through technologies such as motion capture, avatars and AI, where are such qualities lost or changed, and what does that change for the dancers, choreographers and audiences who work with the result?

Approach

Making and testing work with these systems in the studio, and reading what the data records against performance analysis and dancers’ own accounts of moving.

The last signals of humanity, resonating in chaos.

The end of humankind, read through communication. As the environment collapses, bodies become biological antennae whose nervous systems rewire, sending signals no one can yet decode.

MovementMechanical everyday phrases break into trembling, antenna-like gestures, split into each dancer’s own signal, then gather into one body that keeps transmitting.

A Splendid World주옥같은 세상

  • Nov 7, 2023

A comedy from far away, a tragedy up close.

Prompted by the possibility of a Mount Baekdu eruption. The dancers are volcanic ejecta that break out of the earth, roam the air and come upon a human.

MovementFloor-bound shaking and bouncing before the eruption gives way to group movement across the whole space, until a human appears.
