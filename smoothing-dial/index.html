Smoothing Dial

Pose estimation smooths a dancer's tracked joints so the avatar does not jitter. The same smoothing removes the short pauses and sharp accents that give a phrase its weight. Turn the dial and watch what the avatar keeps.

The filter is a causal exponential smoother, the kind real-time tracking uses because it cannot see the next frame. Stronger smoothing steadies the avatar but delays it and lowers its peaks. To be read, the dancer can move bigger and slower, or change the setting.

The demo phrase is synthetic: a held breath, a fast reach, a short upper-body accent, and a rise that speeds up. A loaded clip is analyzed in your browser with MediaPipe Pose Landmarker and does not leave your device. Clips are read up to 60 seconds.
